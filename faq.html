Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about NexSkill services, pricing, courses and contact options.

What does NexSkill offer?

Digital marketing support, creative design, video editing, website/landing-page support and learning topics. Availability is confirmed when you enquire.

What is the starting monthly price?

Selected service plans start at ₹3,500/month. Final price depends on scope, deliverables and any third-party charges.

Is ad spend included in the service price?

Not automatically. Advertising spend, software, hosting, domains and other third-party fees should be listed separately in your quote.

Do you guarantee sales or viral reach?

No. Digital results depend on the offer, audience, budget, platform and other factors. We do not guarantee sales, followers or viral reach.

How do I get started?

Use the Contact page to prepare an enquiry, then continue the conversation through Telegram.

Does the website contact form save my information?

No. This static website has no server or database. The form opens the Telegram group and tells you to send your enquiry manually; it does not transmit or store your form submission.

How do I ask about courses?

Visit Courses or Contact and ask for current details about format, syllabus, duration and fees before enrolling.

Can I request a custom package?

Yes. Describe your goals and requested deliverables; a suitable scope and quote can be discussed before you commit.
